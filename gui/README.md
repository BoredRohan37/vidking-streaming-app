MovieBox  -  desktop app
========================

A fast, good-looking way to browse and watch. Built for Windows 7 SP1, 8.1 and
newer. It draws everything on the CPU, so it needs no graphics card or OpenGL.

GETTING STARTED
  1. Put mpv.exe in the "tools" folder (see tools\README.txt). A Windows
     7-compatible build of mpv is needed for playback.
  2. Double-click MovieBox.exe.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
  - Browse trending, popular and top-rated movies and series.
  - Search with suggestions (press / to jump to the search box).
  - Switch source any time with the pill at the top right.
  - Continue Watching, My List, and up to five profiles.
  - "Up next": when an episode ends you are offered the next one.
  - Download streams, or watch Live TV from your own IPTV (M3U) playlists.
  - Pick a maximum quality in Settings to save data on older PCs.

AI FEATURES (optional)
  Add your own Anthropic API key in Settings to unlock:
    * Ask AI  - describe a mood or a favourite and get matching picks.
    * Picked for You - personal recommendations from what you watch and save.
    * Why watch? - a short, spoiler-free pitch on any title.
  The key is stored only on your PC and is sent only to the AI service, and
  only when you use an AI feature. Family-friendly profiles keep AI picks
  suitable for children.

KEYBOARD
  /       search          Esc     back or close a menu
  Enter   run search or AI request

YOUR DATA
  Profiles, My List, history and settings are saved in gui_store.json inside
  the MovieBox config folder. If the app ever crashes, details are written to
  gui_crash.log in the same folder.
